What is TrimRx?

TrimRx is an online weight loss program. You fill in a health questionnaire, a licensed provider reviews it, and if treatment is right for you, your medication is delivered to your door. In this Trim Rx review we keep things short and clear.

How TrimRx works

  1. Answer the online questionnaire about your health and goals.
  2. A licensed provider reviews it and decides if treatment is suitable.
  3. Get your plan. If approved, your prescription is filled by a partner pharmacy.
  4. Receive it at home and stay supported with check-ins.

How well does TrimRx work?

Results depend on the medication, the dose, your habits and your body. In large trials of the FDA-approved medicines, semaglutide users lost around 15% of body weight on average over about 68 weeks, and the highest tirzepatide dose reached up to roughly 20% over about 72 weeks, together with diet and activity changes.

These numbers come from trials of approved brand-name medicines, not from TrimRx itself. Compounded versions are not FDA approved. Your results will vary and no one can promise a number.

Is TrimRx legit or a scam?

TrimRx uses a telehealth model with provider review and a pharmacy, which is a good sign. Before you sign up, quickly check that:

  • The provider is licensed in your state
  • A pharmacy fills your prescription
  • Pricing, renewals and refunds are clearly stated
  • Independent reviews are mostly consistent
